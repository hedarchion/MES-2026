☀️ Solar Energy
Solar energy is energy from the sun.

The sun produces 173,000 terawatts of energy constantly — that's 10,000 times more than the world's total energy use.

Solar panels capture this energy and convert it into electricity using the photovoltaic effect.

Key Terms
TermMeaning
Photovoltaic (PV) cellDevice that converts sunlight directly into electricity
PhotonParticle of light / energy from the sun
ElectronNegatively charged particle in an atom
Solar PanelA collection of PV cells wired together
Photovoltaic EffectProcess where light knocks electrons loose, creating electric current
