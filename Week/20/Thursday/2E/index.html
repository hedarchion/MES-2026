1 How is sunlight converted into electrical energy in a solar panel?
Photovoltaic (PV) cells absorb photons from sunlight, knocking electrons loose and creating an electric current — this is called the photovoltaic effect.
2 Name TWO factors that affect how much electricity a solar panel can produce.
1. Light intensity / amount of sunlight
2. Temperature (panels lose efficiency above 25°C)
3. Angle of panels toward the sun
4. Shading or cleanliness of panels
