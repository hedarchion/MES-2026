Supporting vs Contrasting Devices
TypeDeviceExample
✅ Supportingfurthermore"He is smart. <b>Furthermore</b>, he works hard."
✅ Supportingmoreover"The car is fast. <b>Moreover</b>, it is safe."
✅ Supportingin addition"She sings well. <b>In addition</b>, she dances."
✅ Supportingalso"I like maths. I <b>also</b> enjoy science."
⚡ Contrastinghowever"It was raining. <b>However</b>, we went out."
⚡ Contrastingon the other hand"Cats are quiet. <b>On the other hand</b>, dogs are loud."
⚡ Contrastingalthough"<b>Although</b> tired, she kept working."
⚡ Contrastingwhereas"He likes tea, <b>whereas</b> I prefer coffee."
