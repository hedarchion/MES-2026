Talking About Time
We use different tenses to talk about when things happen. Today, we will look at two important times:
  • Present Simple: For habits and routines.
  • Present Continuous: For things happening right now.
1/14
2The Present Simple
Habits & Routines
We use the Present Simple for things we do again and again — habits, routines, or facts that are always true.

I walk to school.
She plays tennis.
2/14
3Present Simple Time Words
How often?
  • often → I often read books in the evening.
  • sometimes → We sometimes eat pizza on Fridays.
  • every day → He brushes his teeth every day.
  • once a week → They wash the car once a week.
  • every week → She visits her grandmother every week.

Word Order Tip: Put often and sometimes before the action word. Put every day, once a week, and every week at the end.

6The Present Continuous
Right Now!
We use the Present Continuous for actions happening at this exact moment, or around now. The action is not finished.

Form: am / is / are + action word ending in -ing

I am speaking English right now.
Look! It is raining.
They are studying for their exam today.
6/14
7Present Continuous Time Words
When is it happening?
Instead of talking about habits, these time words point directly to this moment:
  • now / right now → I am watching TV now.
  • at the moment → She is sleeping at the moment.
  • today → We are wearing jackets today because it is cold.

10Compare the Two Tenses
Routine vs Right Now
Present Simple (Routine)Present Continuous (Right Now)
I usually drive to work.Today, I am walking to work.
He plays football every week.Look! He is playing football now.
They sometimes watch a movie.At the moment, they are watching a movie.
