Welcome! Today's Goal
Talking About Time

We use different tenses to talk about when things happen. Today, we will look at two important times:

• Present Simple: For habits and routines.
• Present Continuous: For things happening right now.
1/9
The Present Simple
Habits & Routines

We use the Present Simple for things we do again and again. These are our habits, routines, or facts that are always true.

Examples:

• I walk to school.
• She plays tennis.
2/9
Present Simple Time Words
How often?

We use special "time words" to show how frequently we do these habits.

• often → I often read books in the evening.
• sometimes → We sometimes eat pizza on Fridays.
• every day → He brushes his teeth every day.
• once a week → They wash the car once a week.
• every week → She visits her grandmother every week.

Word Order Tip: Put "often" and "sometimes" before the action word. Put "every day", "once a week", and "every week" at the end of the sentence.

The Present Continuous
Right Now!

We use the Present Continuous for actions that are happening at this exact moment, or around now. The action is not finished.

Form: am / is / are + action word ending in -ing

Examples:

• I am speaking English right now.
• Look! It is raining.
• They are studying for their exam today.
5/9
Present Continuous Time Words
When is it happening?

Instead of talking about habits, these time words point directly to this moment:

• now / right now → I am watching TV now.
• at the moment → She is sleeping at the moment.
• today → We are wearing jackets today because it is cold.
