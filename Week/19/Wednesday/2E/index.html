Solar Energy — True or False?
Tap each statement to check your answer • Green = Correct • Red = Wrong
1 Solar panels make electricity because they get very hot from the sun's warm rays.
❌ Solar panels use light, not heat, to make electricity. They work better when it's cold and sunny rather than hot and sunny!
❌ WRONG
2 Solar panels can still make power on cloudy days, but they make less than on sunny days.
✅ Sunlight can still go through clouds. It's not as strong, so the panels make less energy, but they don't stop working completely.
✅ CORRECT
3 Some large solar power stations use mirrors to focus sunlight, heat up water, and make steam to turn machines that produce electricity.
✅ This is a different type of solar power (CSP). Instead of using normal panels, it uses giant mirrors to catch heat and make steam, just like older power stations.
✅ CORRECT
4 Making a solar panel in a factory creates absolutely no pollution and uses no oil or coal.
❌ Running a solar panel is completely clean. However, building them in factories still requires a lot of energy, mining, and transport, which creates some pollution at the start.
❌ WRONG
