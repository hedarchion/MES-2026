Choppy vs Smooth Writing
🤖Version AChoppy / Robot
I want to play badminton.
It is fun.
I do not have a racket.
VS
✍️Version BSmooth Writer
I want to play badminton because it is fun, but I do not have a racket.

Connectors
ConnectorMeaning / SymbolExample Sentence
1. AND+ (Plus / Add)We can play badminton AND we can eat lunch.
2. BUT! (Problem)I want to go cycling, BUT I don't have a helmet.
3. BECAUSE? (Reason / Why)Let's meet early BECAUSE it is hot at 12:00 PM.
4. SO→ (Result)The movie is at 4 PM, SO let's meet at 3:30 PM.

Fusion 1 · AND (Addition)
We can meet at the lake park.
+
We can buy ice cream after.
💬 Which connector adds two good ideas together?→+ AND!
We can meet at the lake park and we can buy ice cream after.

Fusion 2 · BUT (Contrast)
I want to watch a movie.
+
I have homework.
💬 There is a problem! Which connector shows a problem?→! BUT!
I want to watch a movie, but I have homework.

Fusion 3 · BECAUSE (Reason)
Let's meet at 8:00 AM.
+
The weather is cool in the morning.
💬 Why 8:00 AM? Give me the reason connector!→? BECAUSE!
Let's meet at 8:00 AM because the weather is cool in the morning.

Fusion 4 · SO (Result)
The sports hall closes at 6:00 PM.
+
Let's play at 4:00 PM.
💬 What is the result? Which connector shows the next step?→→ SO!
The sports hall closes at 6:00 PM, so let's play at 4:00 PM.
